# Repository AI contribution instructions

For work in `LearningIndieDev`, follow the project guidance in [`LearningIndieDev/AGENTS.md`](LearningIndieDev/AGENTS.md) and the Studio Guidelines in [`LearningIndieDev/docs/Studio Guidelines/README.md`](LearningIndieDev/docs/Studio%20Guidelines/README.md).

The repository-local Codex hooks under `.codex/` are currently an alert-only prototype. Treat their warnings as visible policy guidance; do not interpret them as automatic blocks or as a replacement for human approval of shared, destructive, external, or irreversible actions.
