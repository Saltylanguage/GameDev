## The first sound target

Forest Edge needs a quiet environmental bed and sparse event cues: one soft
animal-birth sound, dry grass or paw movement for a nearby hunt, a leaf/seed
pluck for a meaningful feed or sprout, distinct hit/escape outcomes, and a short
phase-completion phrase. Routine events must not become a wall of sound at 4×
speed. The GalapagOS Desktop suggests paper, wood, glass, light metal, insects,
and soft field ambience. These are directions to audition against, not final
sound-design decisions. See [the board exploration](SIMULATION_BOARD_POLISH_EXPLORATION.md)
and [Desktop art direction](../GALAPAGOS_DESKTOP_UI_ART_DIRECTION_PASS.md).

The project currently has no imported audio clips or authored AudioMixer;
the Main Menu chime is generated in code. The first practical audition should
complete the existing birth-poof moment before collecting a large library.

## Trial order and acceptance questions

1. Audition one free ambience loop plus one soft birth cue against a natural
   birth on the live board. Does the newborn remain the focus, and is the cue
   tolerable on repeated playback at 1× and 4×?
2. Test grass/leaf movement and a restrained impact as separate hunt and
   outcome layers. Can a player hear escape versus loss without a cheerful
   kill cue or a sound for every tick?
3. Compare Kenney, Willosuna, and lolurio on the Desktop's most common click,
   confirm, deny, shop purchase, and phase transitions. Listen for fatigue and
   consistent loudness across families.
4. Use the paid list only to fill a demonstrated gap. Prefer one coherent
   palette over a larger collection of mixed styles.

For any clip selected, preserve its source URL, creator, acquisition date,
license text or screenshot, and attribution line (when required) in an asset
provenance record. Verify the downloaded file matches the listing and review
the actual included license. A store page's "royalty-free" label alone is not
the complete license. Music also needs a streaming/Content ID check before a
commercial release. This research does not approve a purchase or import.
