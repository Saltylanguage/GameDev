# S4-03 Hare strategy screen plan

Status: predeclared screen inputs; 2026-10-11
Source baseline: codex/s4-gestation-checkpoint-20261010 at 65e47386c3769143c5cb89bae2fd59a8facfbd74

## Decision and purpose

Josh selected 400 Plants / 20 Hares / 10 Foxes as the main S4 matched-seed screening fixture on 2026-10-11 after the five-seed gestation-baseline pilot. Retain 400 / 25 / 15 as a separate stress comparison. This selection changes no production Forest Edge asset. The screen asks whether shared skill choices produce recognizable Trailblazer, Warren, and Gardeners behavior under one fixed scenario. It is exploratory and cannot establish final balance.

## Fixed inputs

- Forest Edge production scenario asset with command-line population override; 36x20 grid, 400 Plants / 20 Hares / 10 Foxes at tick zero.
- Hare player focus; six continuous 100-tick phases (600 scheduled ticks), 0.1-second step, no reinforcement purchases.
- OpposedRoll combat, Natural attack opportunities, bev-experimental metrics, current committed skill values/caps and 48-tick animal gestation.
- Coupled Fox responses off. Fox rules remain fixed across arms.
- Twenty matched seeds: 11100 through 11119 inclusive. These seeds were chosen before observing any result from this screen and are separate from pilot seeds 10100-10105.
- All arms start with no skill. A chosen skill is acquired at the phase boundary after tick 100 or 200, stays active for the rest of the run, and later choices are skipped. Early extinction remains in the dataset with its choice-reach limit.
