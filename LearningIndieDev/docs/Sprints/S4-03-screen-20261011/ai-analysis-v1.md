## Executive summary

The screen proves that the current skills can change mechanics under one fixed Forest Edge fixture. It does not yet show three readable, distinct strategies. Trailblazer moves more, but broad coverage and lower crowding have not been measured. Warren's S4 skills face little crowding pressure and cannot stand in for the later Burrows identity. Gardeners reliably plants at the approved reserve cost, but local patch renewal and player-visible movement remain unmeasured. Survival differences fluctuate on this 20-seed panel and should not drive a value change by themselves.

## Findings and confidence

1. **High confidence — Threat Avoidance now acts.** Both code paths that disabled it were found and fixed. A zero-flee-speed combat regression passes, and the corrected arm differs from control across matched seeds. The lower total Fox attack count is an observation, not proof of lower per-Hare exposure; runs have unequal lengths and populations.
2. **High confidence — General Movement increases movement activity.** The single-skill rate was 1.7632 versus 1.4286 in control; both corrected Trailblazer orders were also above control. The report lacks distinct-cell coverage, travel distance, and crowding-exposure denominators, so the requested speedster identity is only partially evidenced.
3. **High confidence — Seed Dispersal performs its immediate transaction.** The single arm made 270 drops and spent 270 reserve units. The two Gardeners orders made 152 and 301 drops. Patch formation, later consumption, recovery cycles, and readability remain uncertain.
4. **Medium confidence — this fixture under-exercises Warren crowding.** Control lost 13 total crowding-energy units across 20 runs. That is too little to judge the approved density surcharge or Crowding Tolerance tradeoff. Without Burrows, this screen cannot validate the intended settled reproduction path.
5. **Low confidence — no arm has an established survival advantage.** At tick 600, control was 6/20; corrected Avoidance 4/20, Movement 7/20, the two Trailblazer orders 6/20 and 7/20, both Warren orders 5/20 and 6/20, both Gardeners orders 5/20. The paired gain/loss counts and early extinctions show substantial seed dependence. Do not tune from this small panel alone.
