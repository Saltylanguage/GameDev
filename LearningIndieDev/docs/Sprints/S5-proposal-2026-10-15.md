# Sprint 5 proposal — Forest Edge simulation and upgrades

> **Status:** Proposed for review on 2026-10-15. This is not a kickoff or a
> Trello commitment. Candidate dates: 2026-10-15–2026-10-28. Planning capacity:
> Josh 20h and Sim 20h, including an 8h integration and review reserve.

## One outcome

**Integration note, 2026-10-16:** The
[combined Field Data/simulation planning draft](../FIELD_DATA_AND_SIMULATION_ALIGNMENT.md)
reconciles this proposal with Bevin's reviewed first-slice rules and technical
drafts. Field Data prices, controls, rewards and its test route are now defined;
permanent content and the sprint selection remain open. The candidate text below
retains the October 15 proposal and is not a second design backlog to implement.

Make the first Forest Edge/Hare journey's simulation pressure and temporary
upgrade choices understandable and meaningfully different in actual play. Use
the current three Hare build directions—Trailblazer, Warren, and Gardeners—as
comparison lenses, while allowing players to mix skills freely.

The next work block should be spent on simulation behavior, authored upgrade
effects and values, matched evidence, and player review. The profile foundation,
Desktop launch route, journey shell, and local save format are already pushed on
`ProjectMain`. Economy payout amounts, phase-clear gates, and permanent upgrade
content still need design and belong with this simulation and upgrade work.

## Candidate work, in dependency order

1. **Freeze the current-code playtest inputs and observations.** Bevin chooses
   provisional phase-clear judgments for initial playtests. Record the actual
   production and research fixtures separately, including board, populations,
   step, seed panel, offer timing, and the visible behaviors that will count as
   evidence. The previous 400/20/10 screen and 400/25/15 stress fixture are
   research inputs; neither silently changes the 400/55/35 production asset.
2. **Correct the agreed simulation pressures before tuning numbers.** The
   approved crowding direction begins at four neighboring Hares, while the
   implemented rule still uses the older seven-neighbor threshold. Flat move
   energy costs and the whole-litter birth retry rule have provisional
   directions but still need implementation and measured effects. Select the
   smallest coherent slice that can be compared without mixing causes.
3. **Tune the three shared-skill paths in the actual player route.** Check
   Trailblazer travel, threat avoidance, food access, and dispersion; Warren's
   local survival and reproduction; and Gardeners' patch renewal, movement
   between patches, and stored-food cost. Keep intermediate outcomes and the
   exact skill acquisition order visible, not just final populations.
4. **Design the first permanent and within-journey upgrade content.** Choose
   the first playable Genome node and its species/biome counterplay, price and
   unlock condition; define the first Field Data purchase costs and limits and
   the success-only permanent reward categories. Then connect the approved
   rules to the existing profile and journey persistence boundaries. The
   hypothetical 100-mating Burrows unlock is not an approved threshold.
5. **Review one playable vertical slice.** Bevin and Sim record accept/revise
   decisions against the frozen inputs, with separate Species and Biome
   scorecards where an upgrade affects both. Any claim beyond those inputs
   needs a new comparison.

## Existing boundaries

- A failed journey forfeits its run currency and temporary upgrades; permanent
  Desktop upgrades and feature availability remain on the profile.
- Field Data carries across phases and nodes within a journey. Permanent data
  can be banked only on whole-journey success; the categories, amounts, and
  trigger rules remain unapproved.
- The journey's first ten markers are playable. Later markers and final
  panorama artwork are content/art follow-ups, not evidence of a complete
  thirteen-encounter journey.
- Active-run disk resume, cloud sync, extra species, and broad presentation
  polish are outside this proposed primary outcome.
