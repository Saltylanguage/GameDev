# Team retrospective — meeting plan (draft)

**Status:** Planning only. No retrospective conclusions or process changes have been agreed.
**People:** Josh, Sim, Chrono.
**Suggested length:** 60 minutes.
**Scope to confirm:** Start with Sprint 3 and the M1 closeout (closed 2026-09-29), including simulation, art, integration, and collaboration. Josh may choose a broader recent-work scope before the invitation is sent.
**Meeting time and channel:** To be confirmed with the participants.

## Purpose

Hear each person's view of what helped the work, what caused avoidable friction, and what the team should stop doing. Leave with a small number of agreed changes to try in the next work block. This is a review of the workflow and handoffs, not a performance review of people or a second M1 acceptance gate.

## Light preparation

Each person brings one or two concrete examples for each prompt below. An example can be a handoff, test or visual review, decision, or delay. Include what happened and its effect; no slide deck is needed. Examples from Sim's simulation and evidence work and Chrono's art delivery and review path are especially useful because both cross Josh's integration work.

- **Worked well:** What helped us finish, understand, or verify the work? What should we keep?
- **Could be better:** Where did ownership, timing, review, tools, or information cause rework or delay?
- **Stop doing:** What recurring step costs more than it helps, obscures the state of work, or should be replaced?

## Proposed 60-minute agenda

| Minutes | Topic | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Agree the scope, ground rule, and desired result | Shared frame; one person captures notes. |
| 5–12 | Reconstruct key S3/M1 milestones and handoffs | Common timeline, with disputed facts marked for follow-up. |
| 12–25 | What worked well? | Practices worth keeping, with examples. |
| 25–38 | What could be better? | Friction and likely causes, separated from proposed fixes. |
| 38–47 | What should we stop doing? | Specific steps to stop, simplify, or replace. |
| 47–57 | Pick up to three changes to try | Each has an owner, next check date, and observable sign it helped. |
| 57–60 | Read back agreements and unresolved questions | Shared notes and next steps. |

Let everyone contribute before ranking themes. If a topic needs design or technical investigation, assign a follow-up instead of trying to resolve it in this meeting. Do not turn a suggestion into a studio policy or S4 commitment without the relevant owner review.

## Record after the meeting

Capture a concise **keep / improve / stop** summary, examples or links that support each theme, any differing views, and up to three experiments with owner and review date. Update the S4 draft or other project records only for decisions the owners actually accept. Keep raw conversation notes out of the repository.

## Scheduling checklist

- Confirm scope, date, time zone, and meeting channel with Josh.
- Check Sim's and Chrono's availability and invite them with this agenda.
- Share the brief in advance; ask for short examples, not extensive preparation.
