# Rabbit–Fox balance index

**Purpose:** Fast retrieval for future Rabbit and Fox balance work. This is a navigation index and compact evidence summary, not a replacement for the factual reports.
**Owner:** Josh. **Updated:** November 19, 2026.

## Current read — start here

- **Species naming:** the current player-facing and simulation species ID is `rabbit`; historical reports may still call it Hare.
- **Current first-pass Forest Edge (untested):** 400 Plants / 3 Rabbits / 2 Foxes, 34×19, bounded edges, 600 ticks. The current design carries surviving played Rabbits' identity, state, and event history across nodes; Foxes and other nonplayed species/resources reset to authored counts. Offspring may inherit a tunable share of parental stats/effects; exact inheritance remains open. See the [cozy direction](../Sprints/S6-control-record.md#november-18-cozy-individual-led-simulation-direction).
- **Current first-pass species values (untested):** Rabbit starts at 24 energy, has 48 maximum energy, 2 health, 9 vision, and a population cap of 15; Fox has attack 1, vision 8, starts at 48 energy, has 96 maximum energy, loses 2 per 25 ticks, and forages below 72. One Fox hit now wounds a full-health Rabbit; a second successful hit is required to kill it. Fox awareness and Rabbit warning ranges are nearly matched, and Rabbit movement remains faster (2.2 vs 1.0).
- **Population ceiling and crowding (untested):** the Rabbit cap applies to living Rabbits, hidden burrow occupants, and pending litter when reserving births or accepting additions. Its crowding surcharge scales linearly with visible plus hidden Rabbit count / 15, from 20% at the 3-Rabbit opening to the full authored surcharge at 15. The shared `maxPopulation: 0` cap remains disabled, so this Rabbit rule does not cap plants. Josh's setting is recorded in the [Nov. 19 first-pass balance decision](../Sprints/S6-control-record.md#november-19-first-pass-rabbit-population-ceiling-and-crowding).
- **Current carcass pass:** a lethal Rabbit attack creates a shareable carcass with 96 energy. Foxes start at 48, max at 96, lose 2 energy per 25 ticks, and forage below 72. This yields one Rabbit's energy for two Fox maintenance budgets over a 600-tick node; it is only calorie arithmetic.
- **Best current no-upgrade evidence:** the approved carcass experiment completed 100 matched seeds for each 2:1 scale arm. At 2 Fox/1 Hare, 4/2, and 6/3, a seed recorded at least one Fox starvation death in **47%, 82%, and 100%** of runs. Every Hare was extinct by tick 600 in these 300 runs. The fixed-six-Fox 6/6 control had 6/20 seeds with Fox starvation and 15/20 Hare extinctions. There is **no approved universal Fox:Hare survival ratio** and these data do not establish a linear or exponential scaling law.
- **Read next:** the [factual carcass report](Experiments/S6-Fox-Carcass-Value-20261111/REPORT.md), then its [separate analysis](Experiments/S6-Fox-Carcass-Value-20261111/AI_ANALYSIS.md). Exact per-seed data and provenance are linked from the report.
- **Prediction boundary:** the ecology research program is closed as bounded; it has not promoted a general balance predictor. No predictor is calibrated to the current carcass rule. Use forecasts only within their recorded feature/model/held-out scope, and check balance recommendations with matched runs. See the [P3 gate decision](P3_GATE_DECISION_2026-09-06.md) and [Research status](README.md).

## Retrieval rules for balance tasks

1. Use the study table below to choose evidence that matches the question.
2. Read the linked **factual report** for measured values and its **analysis** for interpretation; do not quote the analysis as raw data.
3. Check the listed population, map edges/size, node lifecycle, Fox energy model, tick horizon, upgrade schedule, and seed count before comparing studies. Different rows are separate experiments, not one pooled population curve.
4. Open raw artifacts only for the chosen study when a per-seed, phase, or event-timing question requires them. Large artifacts are under ignored local `LearningIndieDev/artifacts/` folders and may not exist in a fresh checkout.
5. Record any new balance decision separately from the report and AI recommendation, as required by SG-001/SG-005.

## Predictions and unrun proposals

- The predictive research portfolio is **bounded and closed**; no generalized Rabbit/Fox survival predictor was accepted. Current carcass results were generated with seeded simulation, not AI forecasting. For a model-based forecast, first confirm its experiment, scenario, features, and held-out calibration cover the current Fox energy/carcass rules.
- The [Oct 17 Rabbit Genome forecast](2026-10-17-Rabbit-Genome-build-forecast.md) and [Nov 9 predator-first forecast](2026-11-09-S6-predator-first-forecast.md) are evidence-based hypotheses, not trained-predictor output or experiment results. The [Genome prerequisite checklist](2026-10-17-Rabbit-Genome-prerequisite-checklist.md) is implementation readiness, not balance evidence.
- Forecasts, implementation plans, and preregistered panels without completed reports are not observations. In particular, the older [15-condition population curve draft](Experiments/S6-Population-Curve-20261111/CONTRACT-DRAFT.md) was superseded before execution; do not cite its dry-run as results.
