## Findings

1. **600 Plants / 40 Hares / 25 Foxes is the first visual-testing recommendation.** The fresh panel produced 10/20 survivors, 12/20 descriptive recoveries and the only two all-three-species finishes among the rechecked presets. Nine seeds survived where 400/20/10 failed, with no reverse survival disagreement. This supports inspecting it as an interesting alternative, not adopting it as a reliable or balanced default. Ten failures and median final Hares 0.5 remain substantial limitations. Confidence: high for the recorded outcomes, medium for visual-testing priority, low for general reliability.

2. **600/20/25 is a useful nearby comparison.** It survived 11/20 with 13 recoveries, but retained all three species in 0/20 and had median final Hares 1. Starting fewer Hares did not simply produce a healthier ecology. Common seeds do not preserve identical occupied cells when initial counts change. Compare its food and predator histories with 600/40/25 rather than attributing the difference to one isolated mechanism. Confidence: high for observations, low for causal explanation.

3. **600/5/10 is a boom-and-collapse stress case, despite the best fresh survival count.** Thirteen runs survived, mean ending tick was 578.3, but mean Hare peak was 340.35 and median final Hares 1. Mean starvation deaths were 364.8, with no all-three finish. Its high eAVI (0.976) describes predator-active exposure, not proof that it escaped danger well throughout the expedition. Low predator populations and early predator disappearance are visible in the histories; resource depletion and later starvation need inspection. Confidence: high for the shape/counters; resource-pressure explanation is a hypothesis, not a causal isolation result.

4. **The five-seed winners were seed-sensitive.** Both 500/5/15 and 600/5/10 screened at 5/5; fresh survival was 8/20 and 13/20. The sparse 200/20/15 candidate fell from 3/5 to 2/20. This is why fresh seeds preceded recommendations. 500/30/25 is also an outlier-sensitive contrast: 6/20 survival, mean final 19.55, median 0, including a 309-Hare finish after Plants and Foxes disappeared. It is an interesting history, not robust coexistence.

5. **Starting populations change the visible stories, but do not resolve ecology balance.** Only six of 1,680 screening runs and two of 140 recheck runs retained all three species at the horizon. Longer duration, shocks, food-reserve telemetry and the intended healthy operating ranges remain untested. Species-rule changes or the complete Mutation paths could change these outcomes, and need separate matched experiments. Do not promote a preset based only on FPO, APS, a high mean, or one attractive screenshot.
