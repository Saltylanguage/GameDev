## Scope limits

The experiment is a no-action control and says nothing yet about player experience or the efficacy, availability, or visibility of avoidance, survival, and faster reproduction Mutations. The three arms share seed numbers, but the grid generator consumes shuffled placement cells in species order; changing the Fox count can shift Hare and Plant positions as well as predator count. Paired differences therefore include a placement effect. The earlier retained-world report remains intact and its node-two outcomes are not comparable to these fresh-node outcomes.
