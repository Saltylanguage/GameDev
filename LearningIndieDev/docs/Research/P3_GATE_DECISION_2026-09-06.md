# P3 gate decision — bounded AI discovery

**Decision ID:** `DEC-P3-0001`  
**Decision:** Close P3 as a bounded research phase with revised exit criteria  
**Decision owners:** Josh + Sim  
**Date:** 2026-09-06  

## EX-003 disposition

**Archive/defer EX-003 as a standalone P3 experiment.** EX-003 has no execution
package, so this decision makes no claim about general AI recommendation
quality, review-time savings, or useful-recommendation rate.

The project does not need to run EX-003 to close this bounded P3 phase. EX-007
already supplied a pre-registered prediction, training and held-out panels,
false-cause review, and a human decision. EX-008 and EX-009 closed the missing
single-variable and same-held-out order comparisons. EX-010 then added accepted
continued-world, phase-aware evidence.

## Gate decision

P3 is closed for the bounded capability demonstrated by the current evidence:

- The simulation can support traceable, held-out, AI-assisted change-impact
  analysis for a declared feature, scenario, metric, and time window.
- The workflow can preserve separate factual reports, AI analysis, and human
  decisions.
- Continued-world acquisition timing can now be measured with phase and final
  Stat-Lines under an approved schedule.

This is a bounded phase closure, not a claim that the system is a generalized
predictive AI tool. Broad recommendation validity, calibration across tasks or
scenarios, balance, fun, and production promotion remain unproven.

## Scope and follow-up

No production balance change or player-facing rule is approved by this gate.
EX-003 may be reopened later as a new human-approved experiment if the team
wants to measure workflow value, recommendation usefulness, or calibration as
their own research question.
