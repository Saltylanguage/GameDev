Product outcome

Deliver a run-based game built around cellular automata as a roguelike. The player guides one species through a journey of simulation nodes and decisions on a map. Each simulation node has six ten-second rounds in one continuing ecosystem. The current implementation carries the played species' population count into the next simulation node and recreates individuals. The November 18 design target is to carry surviving Rabbits' identity, state, and event history across nodes. Nonplayed populations still reset from authored data. Temporary Mutations shape the journey. Successful settlement can fund permanent Gene Lab research and Genome Upgrades for future journeys. The long-term objective is a diverse, resilient ecosystem rather than one dominant species.

The first vertical slice uses:

  • Scenario: Forest Edge.
  • Player species: Rabbit.
  • Opposing species: Fox. Playable plant species are on hold and are not part of the current expedition contract.
  • Introductory Mutation responses to Fox pressure: avoidance, survival, and faster reproduction. Trailblazer, Warren, and Gardeners remain the original build vocabulary, not three accepted playable M2 strategies.
  • Home base: the GalapagOS Desktop. Lab features such as Gene Lab, Species Collection, History, and Expedition Planning live within that player-facing home. The standalone Lab scene remains a legacy/developer route until its retained tests and responsibilities are deliberately migrated.

Josh confirmed Rabbit, Fox, Owl, Deer, and Wolf as the planned playable test roster on November 18. VS-A7 brings them into normal selection and simulation for mechanics testing before balance; the M2 review route above remains pinned to Rabbit. Rabbit uses the canonical species ID rabbit.

Decisions that are already locked

  • The accepted M1 expedition contains six ten-second rounds in one world. The current Field Data journey connects multiple such simulation nodes through map, reward, and shop decisions. Freeze the representative M2 route at S6 review; do not describe one node as the whole journey.
  • The M1 node starts without a Mutation and has decisions after rounds 1–5. The current journey also has node-end reward and shop decisions; terminal results occur when the journey succeeds, fails, or is ended.
  • Mutations are temporary for the current run and exclusive to Species Simulations. Each is communicated with a small icon and keyword, not a full statistic breakdown; its expected impact should be clear in the following round.
  • Pause is available. There is no player Restart of an active journey. End abandons it after confirmation, forfeiting unbanked Field Data and temporary Mutations; already permanent balances and unlocks remain.
  • The M1 one-node victory rule is historical acceptance. For the M2 journey, extinction ends the whole journey without cash-out. The exact authored phase-clear and route-survival expectations remain under S6 review.
  • The Field Data journey has implemented round payouts, shops, and permanent success settlement. Their player-facing balance and M2 result explanation still need review; older proposed performance and Skip-bonus formulas do not override the current economy.
  • Permanent Genome Upgrades are bought with currency in the Gene Lab application on the GalapagOS Desktop and fill a Genome skill tree.
  • Forest Edge board size is deferred; playable plant species, including Fern, are on hold and are not contract requirements.
  • Genome nodes remain unlocked permanently, while each species has a separate active Genome configuration that can change only between simulations.
  • Every participating species receives its frozen active Genome at launch, including species that are not controlled by the player.
  • Versioned local profiles, settlement, Gene Lab purchase/equipment paths, and researched journey-Mutation eligibility now exist. Final catalog prices, some effects, player acceptance, and a real exit/relaunch check remain open; current code is not approval of provisional values.
  • The canonical GalapagOS Desktop route now uses the active profile and a frozen Forest Edge/Rabbit launch request at seed 10100. Focused Main Menu PlayMode checks pass; Sim's launch-input review and a real application exit/relaunch save check remain open.
  • Species and Biome Simulations use separate success scorecards. A result from one mode is not sufficient approval evidence for the other.
  • Active-run disk save/resume is separate from profile, settings, and settled progression saves.
  • Upgrade, species, scenario, and balance work follows SG-005 — Upgrade and Ecology Balance.
  • Research findings remain bounded to their declared scenario, values, seeds, windows, and review decision. EX-010 does not authorize a broader predictive or balance claim.

Explicit holds and non-goals

The following work is not part of the initial vertical slice unless a sprint review deliberately promotes a smaller slice:

  • broad species, biome, scenario, or Genome-tree production;
  • cloud sync or active-expedition disk resume;
  • a generalized modifier, scripting, rule-plugin, event-bus, or navigation framework;
  • full reactive ecology, hidden rubber-banding, or a large random-event catalog;
  • predictive-model promotion or a new research program without a frozen question and human review capacity;
  • final-volume art or audio production before comprehension evidence approves the direction;
  • colony construction, ant tunnels, beaver dams, or other geometry-directed mechanics outside a separately approved experiment.

Roadmap review rule

  1. The playable outcome demonstrated and its evidence.

  3. Acceptance failures, defects, and changed product assumptions.
